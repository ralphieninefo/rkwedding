Privacy Policy

Wedding Venue Control Center is a private, personal-use application for organizing wedding venue research and correspondence.

Information the app accesses

With the Google account holder's permission, the app may access Gmail to send venue inquiries and read relevant venue replies. Relevant message metadata, message text, and attachment metadata may be processed and stored.

How information is used

Google user data is used only to send requested venue outreach, track responses, extract quote details, create English-language summaries, and help the owners compare venues. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared with data brokers.

Processing and storage

The application runs on DigitalOcean and stores application records in a managed database. Relevant correspondence may be sent to DigitalOcean Serverless Inference for automated extraction, translation, or summary. Access to the private control center is restricted.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept only while needed for the wedding venue search. The owners can delete stored application records and revoke the app's Google access at any time from their Google Account security settings.

Google API data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
