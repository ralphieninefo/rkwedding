Private planning tool

One place to manage our wedding venue search.

Wedding Venue Control Center is a private application used by Raphaël and Kasia to organize venue outreach for their wedding.

What it does

  • Stores venue contact details and tracks outreach status.
  • Sends venue inquiries through an authorized Gmail account.
  • Reads relevant replies and text-based quote attachments.
  • Summarizes venue information so prices and options can be compared.

Who can use it

This is not a public service. The control center is password protected and intended only for its owners. Google account access requires explicit authorization by the account holder.

Learn how Google data is handled in our Privacy Policy.
